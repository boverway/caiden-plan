The 1,000-cal shake: 2 cups whole milk + 1 scoop whey + 1 cup Greek yogurt + 2 Tbsp peanut butter + ½ cup oats + 1 banana. This is your go-to fix when the scale stalls.

Easy calorie boosters: whole or chocolate milk, bagels with cream cheese, peanut butter on everything, trail mix, cheese sticks, jerky, burritos, rice bowls, pasta with meat sauce, potatoes, rotisserie chicken. Backpack kit: 2 protein bars, jerky, trail mix, a shelf-stable protein milk.

Weekly groceries (your share): 2–3 dozen eggs · 3–4 gal whole milk · Greek yogurt · cheese · chicken thighs, ground beef, deli turkey · rice, pasta, oats, bagels, bread · peanut butter · bananas, apples, frozen berries · frozen veggies · whey + creatine.

Adjust every 2 weeks: gaining under 0.5 lb/week → add 250–300 cal/day (half a shake). 0.75–1.25 → change nothing. Over 1.5, or waist up more than 1 inch a month → cut 200–250 cal of junk. Supplements: creatine 3–5 g/day (tested brand, doctor OK first) and whey as backup food. That's it.

Rules: Technique first. Leave 2–3 reps in the tank (no failure on big lifts). When every set hits the top of the rep range, add 5 lb (upper) or 5–10 lb (lower). Keep conditioning short (sprints, shuttles), not long runs, to protect the weight gain.

Holiday Block: Sat Dec 19 → Sun Jan 3 (16 days, no school)

  • Add a 5th session on Thursday: speed + skill, or a short upper-body/neck lift. Sunday stays a full rest day. Christmas and New Year's Day are skill-only.
  • Add a 4th meal (about 500–600 cal, like 2 bagels with PB + milk) for about 3,900–4,100 cal/day. Target +2–3 lb over the break.
  • Sleep 9.5–10 h with a steady wake time (8 am, not noon). Daily: 300 wall-ball reps + 15 min of stick work.
  • Back off to 4 sessions if sleep drops under 9 h, joints get sore, or lifts go backward. More isn't better if you're not recovering.
  • PR test Sat Jan 2 (with a coach): 3-rep max on squat, trap-bar deadlift, and bench, plus pull-ups, 10-yd, 5-10-5, vertical, and weight. The deload follows right after.

Weight checkpoints (7-day average): 145 by Nov 22 · 148 by Dec 20 · 150–151 by Jan 3 · 155 by Jan 31 · 157 by Feb 14 · 160 by Mar 14. Grow an inch? Add about 5 lb to the later targets.

In season (from Mar 15): lift 2×/week (35–45 min, heavy but short), and eat more, not less. The goal is to hold or slowly gain.

Grow & Recover (you're still growing)

  • Sleep is when you grow. Most growth hormone is released in deep sleep early in the night, so 9–10 hours isn't optional.
  • Never under-eat. Low fuel can slow growth and bone building. If the weight drops or you're always tired, add food first.
  • Bones: about 1,300 mg calcium a day (3–4 servings of milk, yogurt, or cheese) and 600 IU vitamin D. Michigan winter sun is too weak, so ask the doctor to check your vitamin D level.
  • Lifting doesn't stunt growth when it's coached and done with good form (AAP). The risks are sloppy technique and max-outs without a spotter.
  • Growth-spurt warning signs: pain below the kneecap (Osgood-Schlatter), heel pain, or low-back pain when arching for 2+ weeks. Cut jumps and sprints by half, swap in box squats and trap-bar work, and see the athletic trainer.

10-minute mobility before bed: couch stretch 2 × 45 s/side · strap hamstring stretch 2 × 45 s/side · wall calf stretch 2 × 30 s · 90/90 hip switches × 8/side · open-book T-spine × 8/side · deep goblet squat hold 2 × 30 s.

Recruiting (short version)

  • Film + grades + testing numbers (10-yd, 5-10-5, vertical) travel to any position. Keep your highlight reel under 5 minutes, with the best clips first.
  • D1 men's lacrosse: coaches can't call, text, or make offers until the Wednesday after Labor Day of junior year (NCAA, new for 2026-27). Before then, you can email coaches from your own account, fill out questionnaires, go to camps, and make unofficial visits.
  • Coach email (from your own account): Subject: Class of 20__ | Attack/Midfield | Caiden Overway | Holland Christian (MI) | Highlights. Then write 4 lines: who you are (height, weight, GPA), why that school, your highlight and full-game links, and your coaches' contact info. Follow up every 4–6 weeks with new film.
  • Your worth isn't your offer list. Do your part, then trust God with the rest (Proverbs 3:5–6).
